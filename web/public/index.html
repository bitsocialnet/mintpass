Two passes, one contract

Each app sells its own pass, and both run on the same open-source MintPass contract. Prices are the same: $30 for a year or $60 for three years.

5chan Pass

For 5chan, the imageboard with no admins.

  • Vote on which board fills each directory slot.
  • Post on /vip/.
  • Skip the captcha on boards that accept the pass.

Seedit Gold

For Seedit, the community forum with no admins.

  • Vote on which communities Seedit lists by default.
  • Skip the captcha on communities that accept Seedit Gold.

A pass never gets anyone around a ban. Every board and community keeps its own rules and moderators.

How buying works

  1. Open the pass page in 5chan or Seedit.

    You buy from inside the app, for the account you're using.

  2. Pay in ETH from any Ethereum wallet.

    The price is set in dollars and converted at the current rate. Anything you send over the price comes back in the same transaction. You also pay the network fee.

  3. The pass lands in your app account.

    It's minted to the address built into your 5chan or Seedit account, not to the wallet you paid with. Voting and captcha-free posting work right away, with nothing to connect.

  4. Renew whenever you like.

    New time is added to whatever is left, up to 10 years ahead. An expired pass restarts from the day you renew.

Back up your account before you buy

Your pass belongs to your app account and can never be moved. If you lose the account, you lose the pass. Export a backup from the app's settings and keep it somewhere safe.

What the contract guarantees

It can't change hands.
A pass can't be transferred, sold, or burned. It stays with the account it was minted to.
One pass per account.
Buying again for the same account renews the pass you already have.
Nobody can take it away.
The contract has no admin. No one can revoke a pass, hand one out for free, or change the prices.
Priced in dollars, paid in ETH.
The contract reads the Chainlink ETH/USD price feed. If the feed stops updating, sales pause until it's back; passes keep working.
The money funds development.
Every payment goes straight to the address that funds Bitsocial development. That address can do one thing: hand its role to a new address.
Apps check it on Ethereum.
5chan and Seedit read the pass from the chain. No MintPass server sits in between.

Questions

Is this the old MintPass that asked for my phone number?

No. MintPass used to give out a free test NFT after a text-message check. That service is retired. The new MintPass never asks for a phone number or any other personal information.

Can I buy a pass for someone else?

Yes. Anyone can pay for a pass for any account address, and the pass goes to that account.

What happens when my pass expires?

It stays in your account but stops counting for voting and captcha-free posting. Renewing brings the same pass back.

Is my purchase private?

No. Purchases are public Ethereum transactions, so paying from a wallet links that wallet to your account's pass address for anyone to see. Pay from a fresh wallet if that matters to you.

Do I need a pass to use 5chan or Seedit?

No. Reading and posting stay open to everyone. A pass adds voting and saves you some captchas.

Can I get a refund?

No. Pass time is prepaid and can't be cancelled.
